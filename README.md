# DESI Quasar Analysis Pipeline

A pipeline to identify and analyze proximate absorption systems in quasar spectra from DESI data.

## Context

Quasars are the most luminous active galactic nuclei in the Universe, powered by accretion
onto supermassive black holes located at the centers of massive galaxies. Their activity is thought to be triggered by the inflow of large amounts of gas, driven by secular processes or galaxy interactions and mergers. The enormous energy released is expected to strongly affect their environment, including host galaxies and beyond, playing a key role in galaxy evolution through feedback processes.

## Features

- Extraction of quasar spectra from DESI catalogs
- Identification of absorption systems
- Synthetic spectrum generation
- Statistical analysis and visualization
